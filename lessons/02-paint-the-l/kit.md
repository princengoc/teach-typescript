Everything this lesson hands you, and what each one does. Nothing outside

of it is shut to you: open it and read it.

## The robot

The robot itself. One call, one thing done. It lives in
`src/harness/robot.ts`, under everything else you spend.

| Call | What it does | Where from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `robot.paint()` | Paints the square the robot stands on. | new here |
| `robot.step()` | Moves the robot one square forward. | new here |
| `robot.turnLeft()` | Turns the robot a quarter turn to the left. | new here |
| `robot.turnRight()` | Turns the robot a quarter turn to the right. | new here |
