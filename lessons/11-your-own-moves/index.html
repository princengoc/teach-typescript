Lesson 11: your own moves

For four lessons you have spent moves someone else wrote: paintCells, nextRow. Today they are yours.

Part 1 — what a move is made of

Open src/harness/moves.ts. Almost nothing is left in it. Here is the whole of next: 

export function next(): void {
  if (!robot.wallAhead()) {
    robot.walk(1);
  }
}

That is all a move ever was: robot calls under a name. paintCells and nextRow used to sit in that file beside it. They are gone, and the pictures still have to come out.

Part 2 — one name, many calls

Here is the chart loop you already know, running on the list . On the left, the move you called. On the right, what the robot actually did. 

for (const n of room.bars) {
  paintCells(n);
  nextRow();
}

names carried robot calls. That is what a name is for: you say the short thing, the robot does the long thing.

Three rungs. Every move in them is yours to write. Go build.

The ladder

src/exercise.ts is open on the left. Fill it in, top to bottom. Rung 1 you write paintCells. Rung 2 you write backToRowStart and nextRow, and the chart draws itself. Rung 3 a working mess is handed to you: cut it into a move. Save and this panel marks every rung.

Rungs 2 and 3 also call your moves on their own, away from any picture. A move that only works in one place is not a move yet.

Well done! Every move in that picture is yours.

Nobody handed you paintCells or nextRow this time, and the last rung got shorter as you worked on it. That is the trade: name a thing once, spend it anywhere.
